Simple Chili Recipe

This is a simple and delicious chile recipe that you can make at home. It will take about 30-60 minutes to make and is perfect for a quick dinner. I love it because it is super easy to make and has become a family meal that i've had as long as I can rremember.

Amount Ingredient
1 Onion
1lb Ground Beef
1 tsp Chili Powder
2 cans Black Beans
1 28oz can Tomato Sauce
1 cup Salsa

Instructions

  1. Start by preparing your ingredients. Chop the onion, and drain and squash one of the cans of black beans.
  2. Heat oil in a large pot over medium heat, and toss in the chopped onion until translucent.
  3. Add in, and cook the ground beef over medium heat until browned.
  4. Add the chili powder, black beans(both cans), salsa, and tomato sauce to the pot.
  5. Stir everything together, and let it simmer for 20-30 minutes.
  6. Serve hot with cheese, sour cream, and guacamole.
