Simple Granola Recipe

This is an amazing granola recipe that you can make at home. It will take about 15-30 minutes to make and is perfect for a quick breakfast. I love it because it is super easy to make and has become one of my favorite foods for a quick breakfast.

Amount Ingredient
21 oz Rolled Oats
1/3 cup Brown Sugar
1/2 cup Canola Oil
1/3 cup Honey
1 box Grape Nuts (optional)
1/2 cup Coconut Flakes (optional)

Instructions

  1. Preheat your oven to 350 degrees Fahrenheit.
  2. Start by placing all your dry ingredients + honey in a large pan.
  3. Mix everything together until well combined.
  4. Drizzle your Canola Oil evenly over the pan.
  5. Place the pan in the preheated oven for 15 minutes. Once it is done remove it, mix it well, and place it back in for another 15 minutes.
  6. Let it cool before storing in a container of your choice.
